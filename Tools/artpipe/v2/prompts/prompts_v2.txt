[STYLE]  (dán đầu mọi prompt)
-------
Cute soft chibi pixel art game sprite, in the exact style of the attached character reference:
simple big rounded shapes, smooth clean pixel curves (no jagged staircase edges), very few details.
Soft muted colors with gentle shading: 3 to 4 close tones per color, low contrast, light comes from
the top-left. Outline is a DARK PURPLE-BROWN (#1e1630), not pure black, only around the outer
silhouette; inner shapes are separated by color, NOT by outlines. No harsh highlights, no dithering,
no glow, no drop shadow, no texture noise.
Chibi proportions: the head is about 60% of total height, tiny round body, very short stubby legs.
Faces are tiny and simple: eyes are just small dark dots or short dashes, no anime eyes, no shines.
The sprite is about 36 pixels tall, drawn on a pixel grid, each art pixel is a clean square.
Single sprite centered, facing the viewer in a slight 3/4 front view.
Solid flat pure magenta background #FF00FF, no ground, no scenery, no text, no border.

2. ENEMY: đồng bộ với player (inbox/newchar.png)
---------------------------------------------------
Đính kèm Tools/artpipe/v2/work/player_anchor.png. Dán khối [ENEMY] dưới đây (KHÔNG dán [STYLE]),
rồi dán mô tả từng con ở mục 2.1–2.8.

[ENEMY]
Create a NEW enemy character in EXACTLY the same art style as the attached hooded character,
as if both were drawn by the same artist for the same game:
- Same pixel size: the image is about 66x84 big square pixels, the enemy is about 36-40 pixels tall
  (bat and ghost may be shorter), same chunky soft pixel curves.
- Same outline: one dark near-black line around the outer silhouette and only a few inner lines
  where shapes overlap (arms, body folds), same thickness as the reference.
- Same shading: muted dusty colors, 3 tones per color, light from the top-left, soft lighter
  patch on the top-left of the head, no harsh highlights, no dithering, no glow.
- Same chibi body: the head is a big round shape about 55-60% of the height, tiny round body,
  short stubby feet like two small blocks, small round mitten hands without fingers.
- Same face language: eyes are just two small dark vertical dashes or dots, small angled brows to
  show the mood, mouth only if described below. No anime eyes, no shine in the eyes.
- Same view: facing the viewer in a slight 3/4 front view, idle pose, centered.
- Colors must stay readable on a green grass ground, but not neon.
- Solid flat pure magenta background #FF00FF. No ground, no shadow, no text, no other characters.
